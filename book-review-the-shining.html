Review: The Shining

By Denis Tarasenko Oct 5, 2024

Haven’t read many horror books in my lifetime, but I liked “ The Shining”. The beginning and middle parts are very promising and enjoyable because of character building.

The last 200 pages aren’t so good. I even had a thought of giving up the book and move on. Why? The story is very predictable and you can see that the author tried to expand as much as he could so everything he came with before fit together in the end.

It’s my opinion, of course. Many people enjoyed it thoroughly, and even reread it a couple of times. Also I would like to mention that the themes in the book are intense and not for everyone to bear, which is an obvious thing to expect from Stephen King.
