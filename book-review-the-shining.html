Review: The Shining

By Denis Tarasenko Oct 1, 2024

I haven’t read many horror books during my lifetime, but I liked “The Shining”. The beginning and middle parts are very promising and enjoyable because of the masterful character building.

The last 200 pages aren’t so good, though. I even thought of giving up the book and move on. Why? Because the story becomes rather predictable and you can see the author tried hard to expand as much as he could so everything he had come up with before fit together in the end.

As I said, it's my first horror book. Many people enjoyed it a lot, and even reread it a couple of times. Also, I'd like to mention that there are some themes in the book that are pretty intense and might make some people sick, but that's kind of an obvious thing to expect from King.
